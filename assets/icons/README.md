# Fitness tracker icons

Source: https://github.com/tabler/tabler-icons

Outline SVG icons by Paweł Kuna: barbell, run, stretching, salad, moon, settings.
Distributed under the MIT License. See LICENSE.txt.

`achievement-legend.png` is original AI-generated artwork created for FitQuest from the approved FitQuest Legend concept. It has a transparent background and is cached for offline use.

`achievement-platinum.svg` is original FitQuest vector artwork for 100 Active Days, with a faceted platinum finish and sparkle accents.

`achievement-active-7.svg` and `achievement-streak-30.svg` are original FitQuest medal variants with numbered faces, distinct ribbon colours, and laurel/flame details.

All-skills Levels 20, 30, 40 and 50 have progressively ornamented original SVG medals. Regenerate with `python scripts/generate-all-skills-badges.py`; Level 50 keeps the existing `achievement-grandmaster.svg` filename and remains hidden until earned.

Generic Skill Level 10, 25, and 50 achievements use original numbered bronze, silver, and crowned gold crests. Regenerate with `python scripts/generate-skill-medals.py`.

The 3/7/14/30-day streak medals share a red ribbon, flame emblem, and numbered face, escalating from bronze through silver and gold to crowned platinum. Regenerate with `python scripts/generate-streak-medals.py`.

Personal Bests use original achievement-specific artwork: New Weekly Best has a gold trophy and calendar, while On the Rise has a silver-and-green crest with ascending steps and an upward arrow.

Active Days medals share a green ribbon, calendar/check emblem, and numbered face, progressing from bronze to crowned platinum at 100 days. Their deterministic source is `scripts/generate-active-day-medals.py`; the final tier retains the filename `achievement-platinum.svg`.

The refined Legend badge keeps the crown, central star, one wing layer, and all five skill medallions. Its reveal uses a soft glow, a gentle scale-up, and one fireworks burst. Reduced-motion preferences disable these effects.
